# App identity

The website uses the app projects' own artwork, inspected on 17 September 2026.

- `dist/assets/sidequest-icon.png`: copied unchanged from SideQuest's iOS AppIcon catalogue, `Icon-App-1024x1024@1x.png`, the 1024px marketing icon. Mountains and a warm yellow sun on cream.
- `dist/assets/haste-icon.svg`: faithful SVG transcription of Haste's Android `haste_foreground.xml` paths and `haste_icon_background` colour, referenced by the manifest's `haste_launcher` adaptive icon. Two leaves on green. Geometry, colours and stroke widths are preserved.
- `dist/assets/revisen-icon.png`: copied unchanged from Revisen's iOS AppIcon catalogue, `Icon-App-1024x1024@1x.png`. Open book on green.

These are current source-project assets, not evidence of published store listings. Each original app repository remains separate and unchanged. Website corner rounding is presentation only.

The original `dist/favicon.svg` coconut remains Kokonut's mark, as requested. The generated alternative was rejected and is not used by the website. Displayed wordmarks use a capital K.
